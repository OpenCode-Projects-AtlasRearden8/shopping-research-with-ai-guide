# The No-Regret Shopping Manual

A friendly, practical manual for buying the big boring things (laptops, phones, appliances, mattresses) without the sting of a bad purchase.

It was written by Dylan Asdale and published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**, in the same spirit as everything else we make: plain English, step by step, never condescending, and always designed to run on your own machine.

## Who this is for

You, if any of these sound familiar:

- You're about to spend real money on something you'll live with for years, and the review pages all contradict each other.
- You've said "I want a good laptop" (or fridge, or mattress) and then stared at two hundred tabs for a week.
- You'd like to use AI to do the boring reading, but you don't fully trust it yet.
- You've been burned once before, and you'd rather not make it a tradition.

None of this requires being technical. If you can copy, paste, and ask a follow-up question, you're qualified.

## The whole method in seven steps

1. **Turn the vague want into a criteria list.** ("Must" really means must. See [Chapter 1](criteria.html).)
2. **Let the AI summarize the reviews, with quotes.** You gather them; the AI reads hundreds; constrained prompts keep it honest.([Chapter 2](ai-reviews.html))
3. **Fact-check the summary.** Three checks, ten minutes, every single time. Still [Chapter 2](ai-reviews.html).
4. **Distrust politely.** Fake reviews and affiliate-driven rankings are everywhere, and easy to spot once you know the tells. ([Chapter 3](spot-fakes.html))
5. **Read the spec sheet in plain English.** Only a few numbers per category shape your daily life; we translate them all. ([Chapter 4](spec-sheets.html))
6. **Time it.** Sale calendars are predictable, and the right month can beat the right coupon. ([Chapter 5](timing.html))
7. **Ask what you're missing, then buy once.** The pre-mortem prompt and the final gut-check ([Chapter 6](ask-missing.html)).

The [before-you-buy worksheet](worksheet.html) has a line for every step, and the [glossary](glossary.html) translates every term of jargon this manual uses or that a spec sheet will throw at you.

## Two ground rules

**No affiliate links anywhere.** Nothing in this manual earns a commission if you buy something, which is exactly why you can trust the rankings-of-priority here. When we say a thing matters in daily life, it's because it does, not because it pays.

**Keep your private life out of AI tools.** When you paste reviews or spec sheets into an AI chat, that's fine: products aren't personal data. Your name, address, account numbers, and medical details are. This manual will remind you of that in the spots where it's easiest to slip. (And the website you're reading? It's built locally, has no analytics, no trackers, and no cookies. Nothing to leak.)

## How to use this manual

Read [Chapter 1](criteria.html) and [Chapter 6](ask-missing.html) end to end; they carry the most weight per minute. The middle chapters are reference material for the category you're shopping for: dip in, grab what you need, get out.

If you only have twenty minutes: read Chapter 1, fill in the [worksheet's first half](worksheet.html), and run the [pre-mortem prompt](ask-missing.html) before you check out.

That's it. Let's make sure the next big purchase is just a purchase, not a story you tell about a mistake.
